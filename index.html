About

I'm Hailey.

A mechanical engineer turned product strategist who likes to understand how things work from first principles to execution.

Growing up in Denver and studying mechanical engineering at Arizona State set the foundation for my life, but a stint studying abroad at the University of Manchester broke me out of a purely technical bubble and sparked a lifelong curiosity for how global markets operate. Early in my career, I had vast engineering experience from working at proving grounds and manufacturing lines, designing suspension, focusing on market quality, and engine and vehicle dynamics testing at Honda. From all this experience, I realized the hard truth: specs on a spreadsheet don't matter if the real-world human experience doesn't connect.

That end-to-end perspective naturally pulled me toward the business side. Now based in Los Angeles as a Product Strategy Senior Analyst Engineer at Honda R&D, I bridge the gap between heavy engineering and strategy focused on providing value to customers. My work focuses on translating deep technical architecture and emerging off-road and mobility trends into actionable product direction and competitive customer positioning.

As a longtime automotive enthusiast, my obsession with the industry goes way beyond the day job. Outside of work, you'll usually find me talking about cars, entrepreneurship, and strategy. Otherwise, I am probably taking another class to learn something new, traveling, practicing yoga, or hiking.
