About

Hello, I'm Hailey.

I was born and raised in Denver, Colorado, and earned my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Arizona State University as a Distinguished Graduate. During college I studied abroad at the University of Manchester, which fueled my love of travel and exploration.

I'm a strategist and mechanical engineer based in Los Angeles. My career has taken me through vehicle dynamics test engineering to leading cross-functional research and development as a Large Project Lead. That experience drove my move into strategic planning, where I align engineering capability with long-term automotive market strategy to deliver products customers want.

I'm working toward a Professional Engineering license and an MBA in International Business, focused on the intersection of engineering, strategy, and international business. As a SEMA student loan scholarship recipient, I've stayed connected to the SEMA community and to my passion for automotive design, strategy, and the economics behind the industry.

Outside of work, I love learning, travel, yoga, and hiking. My goal is to grow into a Director or strategy-level leader and, eventually, to consult independently as an engineering strategist.
